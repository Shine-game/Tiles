How to play

The idea

Build a symmetrical pattern in two colors and watch it come alive. No timer, no rush. Take your time.

Choose a theme

Before you start, pick what you want to create: a garden, a mechanism or an abstract pattern. Each theme gives your pattern its own look. Bordering tiles fuse together to create more complex and surprising objects.

The start

The board has an odd size, so it always has a true center. The game begins with a piece already placed in the middle. Everything grows from there.

Each turn

  1. You're given a piece made of connected squares.
  2. Rotate it and choose its color, switching between the two colors at will.
  3. Place it on the board.
  4. Watch it fuse with the existing tiles and become part of the pattern.

Watch the tutorial

The one rule: keep symmetry possible

Your pattern doesn't have to be symmetrical right now. It only has to be able to become symmetrical later, along at least one axis or rotation (a mirror line, or a turn around the center).

As long as at least one symmetry survives, you're still playing. A move that breaks the last one ends the game.

The symmetry marker

The marker in the bottom-right corner shows which symmetries are still possible. Watch it as you play. When a symmetry disappears from the marker, it's gone, and when only one is left, every move must protect it.

Save your artwork

You can save your creation whenever the pattern on the board is symmetrical. Possible is not enough: the pattern has to match right now. If you like what you've built, share it with others.

The end

The game ends when the piece you're given has no safe place on the board, in any rotation.

Your jokers

Every game starts with three of each:

  • Undo (×3): take back your last move.
  • Discard (×3): throw away the current piece and get a new one.
  • Erase (×3): remove one square from the board.

Use them wisely. They can rescue a pattern that's heading for a dead end.
