Grow a pattern. Preserve the symmetry.

SIMIS is a relaxing, untimed puzzle game about symmetry and creativity. Place and combine tiles while preserving the possibility of reflection or rotation. Explore different visual themes and build intricate patterns through two- and three-tier combinations. Every arrangement grows into a unique design—yours to save whenever symmetry is achieved.
