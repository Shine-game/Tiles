Privacy Policy for Simis

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Simis (the "Game"), published by Cleve Productions ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you play the Game on Android.

1. Summary

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data about you. The Game does not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that receive information from your device. The only information involved in distributing and paying for the Game is handled by Google Play, as described below.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your existing photos and files
  • Device identifiers such as advertising ID, IMEI or Android ID
  • Analytics, crash reports, usage statistics or behavioral data

The Game contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

3. Data stored on your device and artwork you save or share

The Game saves your progress, settings and unlocked themes locally on your device so that it works as expected. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your Android settings.

Artwork you save. The Game lets you save low-resolution artwork locally. These files are stored in the Game's own folder on your device, are never sent to us, and are removed when you uninstall the Game.

Exporting high-resolution artwork. If you choose to export high-resolution artwork to your device, the Game needs access to your device's files (storage) so it can write the image where you choose. The Game uses this access only to save the artwork you export. It does not read, scan or upload your other files, photos or media.

Sharing high-resolution artwork. The Game also has an option to share a high-resolution image using Android's standard “Share to” menu. This option does not save the file to your device. The image is passed to the app you pick, and what that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not receive the image or any information about it.

4. Google Play

The Game is distributed through Google Play. When you download, update or purchase content in the Game, Google collects and processes certain information under its own privacy policy, which may include your Google account details, device information, country, payment information and download or purchase history. We do not control this processing. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Premium content such as additional themes is purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We do not receive or store your payment details. Through the Google Play Console, we may see limited, non-identifying information about our app, such as total installs, country-level statistics, ratings, reviews you choose to post, and order and refund information needed to provide the purchase and to meet legal and tax obligations.

5. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not sell, rent, trade or share it with anyone.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the Game contains no advertising, accounts or third-party tracking. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Since we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection laws, you can still contact us with any questions or requests, and you can exercise your rights regarding data held by Google directly with Google.

8. Security

Because the Game stores data only on your device and does not transmit it to us, the risk of unauthorized access through us is minimal. Please keep your device protected with a screen lock and current security updates.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add features that change how information is handled. The “Last updated” date above will change, and the current version will always be available on this Privacy Policy page. If we introduce data collection, we will update this policy and the Data safety section of the Game's Google Play listing before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Cleve Productions (cleve.productions@gmail.com), [Postal address, if required in your country]

Website mailing list

If you request a mailing list subscription, Cleve Productions uses your email address to send two messages: your free theme promotion code and a note when Simis launches on Google Play. We do not share your address with advertisers or use it for other messages without asking you first.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing Cleve Productions.
